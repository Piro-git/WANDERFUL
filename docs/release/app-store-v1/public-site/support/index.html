Routes are planning aids. Check weather, closures, trail conditions, access rules, daylight, equipment, and water availability before leaving.

Route planning

Wanderful asks for clarification

Add a specific town, trailhead, or landmark and make the route type clear. A target distance or duration helps with loop routes. Requested views, forest, water, or quiet paths are preferences unless verified route evidence says otherwise.

No route or a network error appears

Check your connection and retry once. Then edit the request to use clearer places or fewer constraints. A routing provider can return no usable path; repeated retries do not guarantee a result.

Stats differ from the request

Distance, duration, and elevation shown for a returned route come from available routing data and are estimates. A viable path may be shorter or longer than your target.

Trail Profile and saved routes

Wanderful V1 has no sign-in, user account, or cloud sync. Trail Profile answers, onboarding progress, and saved routes are local to your iPhone.

  • Edit or reset Trail Profile answers from Profile.
  • Open Saved to view or delete a saved route.
  • Removing local app data does not create or submit an account-deletion request because no account exists.
  • Deleting the app normally removes its local data, subject to Apple’s device and backup behavior.

For off-device backend security records or support correspondence, use the privacy choices page.

Speech permissions

Voice input is optional and starts only when you choose it. If Microphone or Speech Recognition access is denied, type the request instead. To change a permission, open the iOS Settings app, find Wanderful, and update the relevant control.

GPX export

Export opens Apple’s share sheet. Choose the app or person deliberately: a GPX file contains route coordinates, and the recipient handles it under their own privacy terms. Wanderful uses a protected temporary export file and cleans it after handoff and during later app cleanup.

What V1 does and does not do

Wanderful plans hiking, trail-running, and biking route options, including point-to-point and loop routes. Depending on the launch configuration, its research engine may use outdoor evidence to support a recommendation.

V1 does not offer an account, cloud-saved routes, payments, subscriptions, ads, community features, live navigation, background location, route recording, offline maps, live weather, or guaranteed current trail conditions.

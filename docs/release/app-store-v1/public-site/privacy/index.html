Privacy, explained clearly

Your adventure is personal.

Wanderful uses the information needed to build a requested route, protect the service, and help when you contact support. It is not an advertising or cross-company tracking product.

Effective: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} Controller: {{CONTROLLER_LEGAL_NAME}}

The short version

  • Your Trail Profile, onboarding progress, and saved routes stay on your iPhone. Wanderful V1 has no user account or cloud sync.
  • When you request a route, Wanderful may send your prompt, requested places or coordinates, activity, constraints, and preferences to the Wanderful backend so it can plan the route and gather relevant outdoor evidence.
  • The routing provider receives the coordinates, activity profile, and routing constraints needed to calculate route geometry. It does not need your identity or a Wanderful account.
  • Wanderful is designed not to create a prompt-history or exact-location-history feature. Transient processing and bounded security logs are described below.
  • There are no ads, analytics, tracking pixels, or cross-company tracking in this V1 boundary.

Route safety: Wanderful is a planning aid, not live navigation or an assurance of safety, access, legality, conditions, scenery, or water. Review every route before starting.

Information handled by the app

Route planning requests

When you ask Wanderful to plan an adventure, the app and backend process the text you enter, requested start and destination places, resolved coordinates, activity type, target distance or duration, route type, difficulty and other preferences or constraints. The purpose is to understand your request, find relevant evidence, calculate route options, and explain how an option matches your request.

Legal basis: {{ROUTING_LEGAL_BASIS}}.

Information kept on your iPhone

Onboarding state, your optional Trail Profile, and saved routes are stored locally. V1 does not create a Wanderful account and does not sync those records to Supabase or another cloud profile. You can edit or remove them in the app. A user-directed GPX export contains route coordinates and goes to the app or person you choose in Apple’s share sheet.

Optional speech input

If you actively start voice input and grant Microphone and Speech Recognition permission, Apple’s Speech service may process the audio to produce text. Typed planning remains available when you decline. Wanderful does not keep the raw audio or send raw audio to its own backend.

Your choices and rights

You can use typed planning without speech permission, edit or delete your local Trail Profile and saved routes, revoke app permissions in iOS Settings, and decide where an exported GPX file goes. See the step-by-step privacy choices page.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection, and you may withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Wanderful has no V1 account, so there is no account-deletion control; local deletion and requests about off-device support or service records are separate.

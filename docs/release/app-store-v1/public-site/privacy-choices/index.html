Privacy controls

Your data, your trail.

Most Wanderful profile and saved-route information stays on your iPhone. These are the controls available in V1.

No Wanderful account No cloud profile sync

Trail Profile

Open Profile in Wanderful to edit, reset, or delete your Trail Profile answers. The profile is local to your iPhone in V1.

Saved routes

Open Saved, choose a route, and use the delete control. Saved routes are local and are not synced to a Wanderful account.

Speech permissions

Voice input is optional. Open iOS Settings, choose Wanderful, and change Microphone or Speech Recognition access. You can always use typed input instead.

GPX exports

After you share a GPX file, manage or delete the receiving copy in the app, service, or device you selected. Wanderful cannot delete copies held by that recipient.

Off-device service records

Route requests can be processed transiently by the Wanderful backend and routing or evidence providers. Bounded security records may be retained as described in the privacy policy. Support email creates a separate correspondence record.

To request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection where applicable, email {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}. Mention whether your request concerns a support email or service/security record. Do not send identity documents unless the privacy contact explains why proportionate verification is necessary.

Make a privacy request

Why there is no account-deletion button

Wanderful V1 has no sign-in or user-facing account and does not sync the Trail Profile or saved routes. There is therefore no remote Wanderful account to delete. Use the in-app controls above for local data and the privacy contact for any off-device record that may concern you.

Website choices

This static site uses no cookies, JavaScript, browser storage, analytics, tracking pixels, embeds, or contact form. It therefore has no cookie-preference panel. If that changes, the site must be reviewed and updated before the new feature is published.

## Description

Describe the outdoor adventure you have in mind. Wanderful uses AI-assisted planning to understand your request and research relevant context, then a routing service calculates real mapped route options.

PLAN FROM AN IDEA
• Plan a hike, trail run or bike ride.
• Choose a loop or a route between places.
• Ask for a distance, duration and the experiences you want.
• Add optional Trail Profile defaults, or start without personalization.

COMPARE BEFORE YOU GO
• Compare route shapes, distance, estimated time and elevation.
• Read why an option fits your request and review available research sources.
• Keep requested preferences separate from routed facts.
• Save a routed result on your iPhone or share its GPX file.

PLAN WITH CARE
AI and source information can be incomplete or mistaken. Routes are planning aids, not guarantees of safety, access, scenery or water. Check signs, weather, trail conditions, closures and local rules before starting.

Online planning requires an internet connection. Availability depends on resolved places, available paths and provider data; coverage is not guaranteed everywhere. Saved route geometry does not mean the background map has been downloaded.
